Six months, $/MMBtu

TTFHenry Hub$8 threshold

Golar keeps 25% of the FOB price above $8/MMBtu on Hilli and Esperanza, and owns 10% of SESA. Management puts the combined effect at about $100M a year per $1 above $8. Hilli starts in 2027 and Esperanza in 2028, so this is a run rate, not current earnings. FOB here is TTF less shipping, a proxy for what SESA's cargoes fetch.

What a Golar unit means for a gas owner

Fee and price share follow Esperanza's terms, scaled by capacity. 1 MTPA is about 52 million MMBtu a year and needs about 145 MMscf/d of feed gas. Feed gas cost includes 10% used as fuel. IRR is unlevered over a 20 year charter with capex of $2.45bn per 3.5 MTPA, and assumes today's price holds for all 20 years, so treat it as a ceiling when prices are high.
